About Us

Our Story

Kkumi Stationery started in 2026 as a small online shop for students. "Kkumi" comes from a Korean word that means "to decorate". We think nice stationery makes studying more fun.

We sell simple and useful items like notebooks, pens and desk accessories at prices that students can afford.
